BRIEFING | You are RED in a game of Classic Risk.
Mode: Duo. Seats: RED BLU, plus the REB faction.
Starting armies: 40 per player; the REB faction starts with 60.
Victory: eliminate the other player. The game ends the moment one of the two players holds no territory; the REB faction does not have to be defeated.

[AS] Asia, bonus 7: AFG CHN IND IRK JPN KAM MEA MON SIA SIB URA YAK
[AU] Australia, bonus 2: EAU INO NGU WAU

Cards: a set is 3 of a kind, one of each type (INF infantry, CAV cavalry, ART artillery), or any 3 cards including a wild (WLD).
Trade-in values by set number traded in the game: set#1 = 4, set#2 = 6, set#3 = 8, set#4 = 10, set#5 = 12, set#6 = 15, then +5 per set.
If a traded card pictures a territory you own, you gain 2 extra armies that must be placed on that territory.
Your card faces are private to you; every player's card count is public.
